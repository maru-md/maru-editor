title: Setup on a new Mac, Trash, and safer editing with agents
---

Maru now opens cleanly on a Mac that has never run it, and walks you through a short setup. Deleted documents go to a Trash first, and you and your agents no longer overwrite each other's edits.

### New
- **First-run setup** — language and look, your name, your Maru pass, Claude Code, the command-line tool and plugin, and automatic backups. Skip any step; close it and it picks up where you left off; run it again from the command palette (`⌘E` → Setup).
- **Automatic backups** — off, an hourly snapshot on this Mac, or an hourly snapshot in a folder you pick (iCloud Drive or Dropbox keeps a copy off this Mac). It runs even while Maru is closed. Settings → Data & Backup.
- **Trash** — deleting a document moves it to the Trash at the bottom of the rail. Restore it within 30 days; after that it is written as a Markdown file to `~/.maru/archive/` and leaves Maru. Deleting that file removes it for good.
- **Conflicts merge themselves** — when an agent edits a document you are editing, changes to different blocks are merged without asking. You are asked only about blocks you both changed, and only for those blocks.
- **Document locks for agents** — an agent can hold a document while it works on it, so two agents don't overwrite each other. You always win: your saves go through, and the app shows "… is editing this" at the top.
- **Comments button** — the comments panel now has a button next to the tags and the shortcut `⌘⇧M`.
- **Approve suggestions in bulk** — click the boxes, or "Mark All", then "Approve Marked".

### Improved
- Folders in the sidebar can be reordered by dragging (drop on the top or bottom edge of a folder; the middle moves it inside).
- Confirmation dialogs no longer wrap button labels onto two lines.
- The iPhone app downloads a much smaller copy of your documents, shows images and videos from your Mac, and has a Trash in Settings.
- For agents and the terminal: `maru stats` reports on the health of your knowledge base (orphans, broken links, tags, what you haven't opened), and `maru reindex` rebuilds the link and tag index.

### Fixed
- Maru quit at once on a Mac without a `~/.maru` folder. It now creates the folder; if anything else goes wrong at startup it shows what happened instead of quitting silently.
- `maru backup --upload` no longer has a built-in upload destination; set `MARU_BACKUP_DEST` to use it.
- Links with parameters (`maru:<id>?…`) and tables inside lists keep their links and block ids when a document is saved again.
